Before publishing:
1. Replace "Add your business email here" in index.html and privacy-policy.html with your real, monitored email.
2. Verify that the business name, contact person, location, services and privacy practices are accurate.
3. Do not submit this website to Meta as proof of legal registration. Meta may separately require official business documents.

Free hosting:
1. Create a GitHub repository named mohsin-property-system.
2. Upload all three HTML files to the repository root.
3. Open Settings > Pages.
4. Under Build and deployment, select Deploy from a branch, choose main and /(root), then Save.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish and use the public HTTPS URL.
